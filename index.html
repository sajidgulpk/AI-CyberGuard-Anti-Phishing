1. Introduction

AI CyberGuard ("we," "our," or "us") provides mobile security, anti-phishing protection, smishing detection, and banking PIN fraud prevention. We are committed to maintaining user trust through transparent data protection practices complying with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA).

2. Local On-Device Threat Evaluation

All evaluations of web links, incoming SMS text messages, and email payloads occur locally on your Android device. We do not transmit, record, or store your personal communications, banking details, or browsing history on external servers.

3. Advertisements & Google AdMob

This application displays advertisements served by Google AdMob and its mediation partners. These third-party services may collect:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) for ad serving and fraud mitigation.
  • Anonymous device information (device model, OS version, coarse location).
  • Aggregated performance and engagement analytics.

Users can reset or opt out of personalized ads via Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
